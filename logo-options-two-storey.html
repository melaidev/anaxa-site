Two-storey “a” marks

These use the real a from the site’s own fonts — Inter Tight 600 and Instrument Serif — pulled straight out of the font files as outlines, so what you see is the exact letterform, not a redraw.

Judge these at the 16px column on the right, not the big version. That is the size that actually matters for a favicon, and it is where the difference between the bare letter and the tile versions shows up.
