Logo & favicon options

Eight marks for Anaxa. Each row shows the mark large, the header lockup on both candidate backgrounds (paper white = version 1, warm cream = version 2), and how it looks as a favicon in a browser tab.

These are your own marks, not Claude’s. Nothing here needs Anthropic’s design resources — those only matter if you want to display an official Claude Partner Network badge, which has its own usage rules. A partner badge would sit in the footer or a credentials strip; it should never be your logo.
