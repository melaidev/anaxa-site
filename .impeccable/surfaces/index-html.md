# anaxa.ai — single page

Scope: `index.html`, the whole site. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience: founders/CTOs at startups and scaleups, and operations/domain leaders at
mid-market companies. Job: decide within one scroll whether to start a conversation.
Action: the contact form. Proof available: the Claude Partner Network membership and the
Claude Certified Architect credential, and nothing else — no clients, logos, metrics or
case studies exist, and none may be invented.

Constraints: static single page, no build step. The modal contact form is user-pinned
(requested from screenshots) and stays. "Anaxa" in prose, never "anaxa.ai". The
two-storey `a` mark in `brand/` stays. The Claude Partner Network sentence is verbatim
and may not be reworded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A credentialed practice stating plainly what it builds, for a reader who will
scroll once. The page owns the claim that the credential *is* the evidence, and refuses
the arrangement that hides a proof-free consultancy behind a grid of six identical
service cards and a row of borrowed logos.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground (#FBFAF8) under near-black ink (#14140F), with one real
colour — a deep pine (#0F4D3F) that carries links, the credential rule, and the primary
action, never scattered as an accent. Archivo sets display and UI; Source Serif 4 sets
every passage meant to be *read*, so register changes with purpose rather than size.
No monospace, no numbered sections, no eyebrow labels, no cards. Hairline rules and
space do the dividing. Recognisable with all content removed by: paper ground, pine
rule, serif prose against grotesk headings.

STORY: The visitor learns Anaxa builds custom AI systems, that an Anthropic-credentialed
architect designs each one, and what six kinds of work that covers — then writes. The
non-technical reader believes it is safe to approach; the technical reader believes
there is real substance. Both reach the same form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width paper. Wordmark top-left, contact link top-right. The
headline sits on a 10-column measure at roughly 64px, two lines, no label above it. One
serif paragraph beneath at reading measure. Below that, separated by a pine hairline and
generous space, the credential stated as a sentence — not a badge — with the primary
action immediately under it, left-aligned. No hero image, no stats, nothing centred.

FORM: The category standard, taken deliberately as the standing exit after the direction
round (seed key 3dd9a789); the user chose convention over a replacement world and named
Anthropic, OpenAI and Scale as the craft bar. Executed at full fidelity, without irony or
smuggled quirk. Signature interaction: a single authored moment — the contact form
resolving in place on submit rather than swapping to a success screen. Motion grammar:
exponential ease-out from an already-visible default; nothing animates on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Unresolved

Whether the site ever gains a second page. Nothing here assumes one.
