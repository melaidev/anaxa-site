00  /  About

Anaxa is a registered member of the Claude Partner Network. Every system we build is designed by a Claude Certified Architect, so it follows Anthropic’s own best practices for building on Claude.

We use deterministic software and LLMs together to build systems that are flexible where they need to be and predictable where it matters, with people kept in the loop rather than replaced by a black box.

We’ve shipped AI products in both B2B and B2C settings. Some worked, some didn’t, and all of it shaped how we work now: applied AI product development, end to end, from first prototype to something that holds up in production.
