Layered “a” marks

The layers idea applied to the three that survived at 16px — tile (01), framed (06) and bracketed (07) — plus a few other ways to read “layers”. Real Inter Tight 600 letterforms throughout.

Layering costs you small-size legibility: a ghost shape at 26–30% opacity is nearly invisible at 16px. Check the 16px column — the ones that still work there keep all their structure in the front layer.
